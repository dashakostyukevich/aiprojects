# Daisy Made — Instagram Design System
Reference sheet based on the branding/template set by Nabi Studio

---

## 1. Color Palette

| Swatch | Name | Hex (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟡 | Mustard Gold | `#E8A93C` | Primary background, most-used card color |
| 🔴 | Coral Red | `#D94F3D` | Secondary background, high-contrast headlines |
| 🟢 | Olive Green | `#7C8B3E` | Accent background, "event" style cards |
| 🌸 | Bubblegum Pink | `#F2A8C7` | Accent background, softer/lifestyle cards |
| 🔵 | Sky Blue | `#A9C9E8` | Pattern/border color, checkerboard motif |
| ⚪ | Cream | `#FBF2DC` | Base neutral — text panels, photo mats, light backgrounds |
| 🟤 | Rust/Terracotta text | `#B5432D` | Headline text on pink/cream cards |
| ⚫ | Dark text | `#2E2A20` | Body copy on light backgrounds |

**Rule of thumb:** each card commits to ONE dominant hue + cream as the neutral. Never mix more than 2 saturated colors per card (the pattern border can introduce a 3rd, sparingly).

---

## 2. Typography

| Role | Style | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline** | Bold, condensed sans-serif, ALL CAPS | e.g. Anton, Archivo Black, League Gothic — does 80% of the visual weight |
| **Accent / tagline** | Handwritten script | e.g. Caveat, Permanent Marker (light weight) — used for "(virtual)", dates, taglines |
| **Body copy** | Clean geometric sans, regular weight, small size | e.g. Poppins, Nunito Sans — low visual priority, factual info only |
| **Logo wordmark** | Rounded script/sans lockup | Stays identical across every post, small circular badge, fixed position |

**Pairing rule:** 1 bold display font + 1 script accent font + 1 simple body font. Never more than 3 typefaces total.

---

## 3. Spacing & Layout Rules

- **Margins:** consistent ~24–32px card padding on all sides
- **Logo placement:** top-left or top-center, same spot every time
- **Hierarchy:** one dominant headline per card — everything else is secondary
- **Photo cards:** white polaroid-style border (~16–20px), slight rotation (−3° to 3°), torn-paper or washi-tape accent in a corner
- **Callout badges:** rounded/irregular blob or starburst shape, used to contain short text (price, date, location)
- **Pattern borders:** checkerboard/gingham strip, used as a full-bleed frame — keep to 2 alternating colors max

---

## 4. Motifs & Texture

- Small repeating line-figure icon (mascot) scattered as background pattern
- Hand-drawn flowers/asterisks near headlines as accent marks
- Rounded "sticker" shapes for short callouts (never sharp rectangles for callouts)
- Slight imperfection is intentional: overlapping elements, small rotations — avoid perfectly centered/symmetrical layouts

---

## 5. Grid Rhythm (Instagram Feed)

- Alternate color-blocked graphic cards with photo-based/scrapbook cards
- Avoid 2 photo cards or 2 same-color cards back-to-back
- Keep 1 consistent visual anchor (logo badge) across every tile so the grid reads as one brand at a glance

---

## 6. Voice Through Design

Warm, communal, handcrafted — small-business/maker energy over corporate polish.
Achieved via: rounded shapes + hand-drawn accents + warm saturated (not neon) palette + imperfect, human placement.
